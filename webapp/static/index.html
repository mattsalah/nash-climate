Realized abatement vs. the Nash optimum

Where a country's marginal abatement cost curve crosses its marginal abatement benefit — and where its observed policy-driven abatement actually sits.

Demo data. These numbers are synthetic and generated for testing the interface. Run without --demo to use output/data/.
Benchmarks

How to read this

  • The MAC curve is a·μ + d·μ4 in $/tCO2, with μ the abatement fraction and a, d the Enerdata coefficients from macc_ed_early.csv.
  • The local MAB is the country-level SCC. Where it meets the MAC curve is that country's best response when it only counts damage to itself — the Nash abatement level.
  • The global MAB is the global SCC; its crossing is the globally efficient (cooperative) abatement level.
  • The vertical bands are realized abatement: emissions avoided relative to a counterfactual that freezes policy at its 2015 level, with the shaded region showing the 95% confidence interval from the counterfactual regression.
  • The cooperative optimum is often far to the right of realized abatement. Set axis scale to Zoom to Nash to rescale the chart around the Nash and realized markers; series that fall outside the window are marked off scale in the legend.
  • Forgone net benefit is the net benefit at the benchmark minus the net benefit at realized abatement, per tonne of BAU emissions: SCC·μ − (aμ2/2 + dμ5/5).
